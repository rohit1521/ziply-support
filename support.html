Ziply Support

Help with ZIP, RAR, 7Z and other archives on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Open or extract an archive

Open Ziply, browse to an archive, and select it to inspect its contents. Choose Extract All or select individual entries. For password-protected archives, enter the password when prompted.

Create a ZIP archive

Select one or more files or folders, choose Compress, give the archive a name, and select the destination. Premium includes batch workflows and password-protected ZIP creation.

Access cloud files

Ziply works with Apple’s Files and File Provider system. iCloud Drive and local storage are available free. Premium enables workflows for Google Drive, OneDrive and Dropbox when their provider apps are installed and configured.

Restore Premium

Open Premium and choose Restore Purchase while signed in to the Apple Account that bought Ziply Premium. A lifetime purchase can be restored on supported devices associated with that account.

Troubleshooting

An archive will not open

Confirm the file has finished downloading, has the expected extension and is not damaged. Some RAR archives may require every part of a multi-part set to be present.

A cloud file is unavailable

Open the provider’s app, confirm you are signed in, and download the file locally if the provider reports it is offline.

A password is rejected

Passwords are case-sensitive. Confirm the archive’s password with its sender and check for spaces introduced when copying it.

Contact

Email support@brightfox.fun with your device model, operating system version, archive format and a description of what happened. Do not attach confidential archives or passwords.
